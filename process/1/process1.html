Model Overview


My solution leverages the BiFPN-YOLOv8 model, a fusion of Bi-directional Feature Pyramid Network (BiFPN) and You Only Look Once v8 (YOLOv8).

BiFPN, introduced by the Google Brain team in 2019, addresses the challenge of uneven contributions of features from different scales, thereby enhancing feature fusion efficiency.

This model comprises a YOLOv8 backbone network, BiFPN feature fusion in the neck, and a head for target detection.



Backbone: It adopted the YOLOv8 backbone network structure, known for its speed and accuracy in object detection tasks.

Neck: The neck of our model uses the BiFPN structure for feature fusion. It receives feature maps from the backbone network and performs multi-scale feature fusion to enhance the detection capability.

Head: The head receives inputs from feature maps at different scales (P3, P4, P5) and outputs the target detection results.
