Dataset



I evaluated this model using the Construction Site Safety Image Dataset, consisting of 2801 annotated images. (Roboflow Universe Projects, 2023)


Data Analysis and Visualization



All images in the dataset underwent uniform preprocessing and augmentation procedures to ensure consistency across different stages of model training.

In data analysis, I visualized the distribution of target box centers and dimensions. For example:

(a) Histogram illustrating the number of instances for each category;
(b) Distribution of bounding boxes for all data;
(c) Histogram of x and y variables, displaying the spatial distribution of the dataset;
(d) Histogram of width and height variables illustrating the dataset’s distribution.
